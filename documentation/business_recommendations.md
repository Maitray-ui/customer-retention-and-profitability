# Business Recommendations

## 1. Protect High-Value Customers

Champions represent approximately 17.1% of the customer base but contribute 62.7% of historical revenue.

**Recommendation:** Prioritise retention initiatives for Champions and Loyal Customers through personalised engagement, loyalty benefits and relevant offers.

## 2. Improve Early Customer Retention

Cohort retention falls to approximately 35.1% by month three and 26.0% by month six.

**Recommendation:** Strengthen the early customer journey through onboarding, post-purchase engagement and targeted follow-ups to encourage repeat activity.

## 3. Reassess Paid Acquisition Efficiency

Paid Ads accounts for approximately 52.9% of acquisition spend but has the lowest contribution per acquired customer among the four channels analysed.

Organic and Referral demonstrate stronger contribution per acquired customer and higher early retention.

**Recommendation:** Review Paid Ads targeting and acquisition efficiency. Investigate opportunities to strengthen Organic and Referral acquisition without assuming their current performance will remain constant as spending scales.

## 4. Address Subscription Cancellation Drivers

Inactivity and Price account for approximately 64.9% of churn-flagged subscription records.

**Recommendation:** Investigate engagement initiatives for inactive subscribers and assess pricing and perceived subscription value.

## 5. Investigate Regional and Subscription-Tier Differences

Basic subscriptions have a higher recorded churn rate than Premium subscriptions. Tier2 also has the highest regional churn rate in the aggregate analysis.

**Recommendation:** Investigate differences in subscriber behaviour, regional customer needs and subscription value propositions before designing targeted retention initiatives.

## Important Considerations

- Findings are descriptive and do not establish causal relationships.
- Historical contribution and supplied LTV should not be interpreted as predictive customer lifetime value.
- Subscription churn and e-commerce purchase inactivity represent different customer behaviours.
- Campaign-level attribution was not established because campaign identifiers could not be reliably matched across the available datasets.
- Statistical testing and predictive analysis remain planned extensions of this project.
