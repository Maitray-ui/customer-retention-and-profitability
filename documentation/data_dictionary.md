
# Data Dictionary

This document provides an overview of the seven datasets used in the Customer Retention & Profitability Analysis project and their business purpose.

| Dataset | Description | Business Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `customers.csv` | Customer profiles, signup dates, acquisition channels and regions. | Understand customer acquisition and customer characteristics. |
| `orders.csv` | Transactions, revenue, discounts, refunds and costs. | Analyse purchasing behaviour and financial performance. |
| `customer_features.csv` | Purchase frequency, average order value, recency and discount dependency. | Analyse customer behaviour and segmentation. |
| `customer_unit_economics.csv` | CAC, contribution, LTV and payback metrics. | Evaluate customer-level economics. |
| `subscription_history.csv` | Subscription tiers, periods, churn and reactivation. | Investigate subscription retention. |
| `marketing_spend.csv` | Marketing expenditure, impressions, clicks and conversions. | Evaluate marketing channel performance. |
| `date_dim.csv` | Calendar and fiscal date attributes. | Support time-based analysis. |
