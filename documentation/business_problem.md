# Business Problem

## Overview
The business operates an e-commerce and subscription model, acquiring customers through multiple marketing channels. However, a growing customer base and increasing revenue do not necessarily translate into sustainable profitability.

The challenge is understanding which customers generate long-term value, where retention is weakening, and whether customer acquisition costs are justified by the financial contribution customers generate.

## Core Business Question
**How can the business improve customer retention and profitability by understanding customer behaviour, subscription churn, and acquisition efficiency?**

## Key Business Objectives
1. **Customer Value:** Identify high-value customers and understand their financial contribution.
2. **Customer Behaviour:** Analyse purchasing frequency, spending patterns, and discount dependency.
3. **Retention:** Evaluate customer retention through purchasing behaviour and cohort analysis.
4. **Subscription Churn:** Examine churn patterns across subscription tiers and recorded churn reasons.
5. **Profitability:** Evaluate customer economics and compare acquisition costs across marketing channels.

## Business Outcome
Provide data-driven insights to support better customer segmentation, retention strategies, marketing investment, and profitability decisions.
